PROJECT ARCHITECTURE & METHODOLOGY

Academic Significance of Welfare Gap Analysis

In public administration, the Welfare Delivery Gap refers to the attrition between statutory scheme entitlement and actual citizen benefit receipt. Traditional government portals fail to quantify where citizens abandon the process.

  • Explainable Rule Engine: Citizens are evaluated deterministically against age, income ceilings, occupation, and social category criteria, producing transparent reasoning.
  • Pipeline Funnel Model: Applications are tracked along Eligible → Aware → Applied → Approved → Received to isolate whether failures stem from awareness friction, procedural complexity, or administrative delays.
  • Data Integrity: All statistics displayed are computed dynamically from real database records (applications, citizens, schemes) rather than mock values.
